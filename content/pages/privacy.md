*Last updated: 10 October 2026*

**Who we are.** Dry & Cosy (dryandcosy.com) is run from Ireland. Contact: see the [contact page](/contact/).

**What we collect.** This site does not ask you for personal data. Our hosting provider may log basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, for security and to keep the site running.

**Analytics.** If we add analytics, we will only set non-essential cookies with your consent, and we will update this page first.

**Affiliate links.** When you click an Amazon link, Amazon may set cookies to track the referral. That is covered by [Amazon's privacy notice](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=201909010).

**Email.** If you contact us, we use your email only to reply.

**Your rights.** Under GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (Ireland).
