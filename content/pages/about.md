Dry & Cosy is based in Ireland. We cover the problems most Irish and UK homes deal with every winter: washing that will not dry, condensation, mould, cold rooms and rising energy bills.

Every guide explains the cause first, then the free fixes, then the products worth buying if the free fixes are not enough. We choose products on what they do for the problem in question.
