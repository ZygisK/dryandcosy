Windows are where a lot of a home's heat escapes, especially old or single glazed ones. You do not need new windows to make a real difference. These cheap fixes work in rentals and take an afternoon.

## 1. Thermal curtains

Lined thermal curtains are the biggest single win. Close them at dusk, tuck them behind the radiator rather than over it, and open them fully in the day to let sun in and let the glass dry.

[[product id="thermal-curtains"]]

## 2. Window insulation film

A clear film stretched across the frame with a hairdryer creates an air gap, like cheap secondary glazing. Ideal for single glazing and draughty old sashes. It peels off in spring.

[[product id="window-film"]]

## 3. Seal the gaps around opening windows

Self-adhesive foam or rubber seal strips stop the whistle and the cold air leaking in around the edges of opening windows.

[[product id="seal-strip"]]

## 4. A thermal door curtain

The front door is often the worst offender. A heavy thermal curtain on a rail inside the door blocks draughts that a brush strip alone cannot.

[[product id="door-curtain"]]

## 5. Radiator reflectors under windows

Radiators under windows on outside walls lose heat backwards. Reflector panels bounce it back into the room.

[[product id="radiator-reflector"]]

## Watch out for condensation

Better insulated windows can trap more moisture. Keep trickle vents open, clear condensation in the morning and keep humidity under 60%.
