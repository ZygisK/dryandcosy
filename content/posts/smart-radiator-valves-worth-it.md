For most homes with radiators, yes. Smart radiator valves let you heat each room only when it is used, so the spare room, hallway and empty bedrooms stop eating heat all evening. They fit onto most existing radiator valves in minutes, with no plumber.

## How they work

A smart TRV replaces the head of your radiator valve. Each one has its own schedule and temperature, controlled from an app. The living room can be warm in the evening while bedrooms only heat up before bed.

## What they save

Savings depend on your house and habits. The biggest gains come in homes where several rooms sit empty for most of the day, which is most homes. Heating only the rooms you use can make a noticeable difference to a winter gas bill.

## Start with a smart thermostat?

A smart thermostat controls the boiler itself, learns your schedule and can turn the heating down when everyone is out. Many households start here, then add smart valves in the rooms that matter.

## Measure first

An energy monitor shows what your home is using in real time, and a smart plug with energy monitoring shows what each heater or appliance costs to run. Both are handy for seeing what actually saves money.

## Plug-in heaters

If you use an electric heater in one room, a simple timer plug stops it running when nobody is there.

[[product id="timer-plug"]]

## Quick verdict

- **Many radiators, rooms empty most of the day:** smart valves, definitely.
- **Small flat, one or two rooms:** a smart thermostat or a good timer is enough.
- **Electric heating:** smart plugs and timer plugs.
