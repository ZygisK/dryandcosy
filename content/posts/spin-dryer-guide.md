A spin dryer is one of the most underrated laundry gadgets. It spins clothes much faster than a washing machine, pulling out a lot more water in a few minutes. Less water in the clothes means faster drying on the airer and far less moisture in your home.

## Why it works

Washing machines spin at up to around 1,400 rpm on a good day, and less on delicate cycles. A spin dryer spins smaller loads much harder. The water pours out of a spout into a basin in front of you, water that would otherwise end up on your windows and walls.

## What it costs to run

Spin dryers use a few hundred watts for a few minutes per load, so the cost is a penny or two. That is far cheaper than any heated method.

## Best way to use it

1. Wash as normal.
2. Spin small batches for 3 to 5 minutes until the water stops.
3. Hang on an airer, ideally heated and covered.
4. Run a dehumidifier in the same room if you have one.

## Downsides

- Small capacity, so you spin a load in two or three batches.
- It needs a sink or basin nearby to drain into.
- It can be noisy and needs to sit level.

## Who it suits

Flats and homes with no tumble dryer, people drying towels and jeans indoors, and anyone fighting condensation from wet washing.
