Black Friday is Friday 27 November and Cyber Monday is 30 November. If you are going to buy one thing for the house this winter, buy it now, while prices on the bigger items tend to drop. This is our short list of what's worth it for a warm, dry home, and nothing that isn't.

## How to tell if a deal is real

- **Check the price history.** Free trackers such as Keepa or CamelCamelCamel show what an Amazon item actually sold for over the last few months. A "40% off" that matches the usual price isn't a deal.
- **Buy what you were going to buy anyway.** A dehumidifier you need at full price is a better buy than a gadget you don't at half price.
- **Look at running costs, not just price.** A cheap heater that costs more each hour can cost more by March.
