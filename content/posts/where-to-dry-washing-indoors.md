The best place to dry washing indoors is a small, warm room with the door shut and either a window cracked or a dehumidifier running. The worst is spread across radiators all over the house, where the water ends up on every window and wall.

## Best spots

| Spot | Why it works | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small spare room or box room | Door shuts, damp stays in one place | Run a dehumidifier or crack the window |
| Bathroom with an extractor fan | Fan pulls the moisture out | Keep the fan on while it dries |
| Utility room or porch | Out of the living space | Can be cold, so use a heated airer |
| Landing or hallway near a radiator | Warm air moves through | Do not block the radiator |

## Worst spots

- **Draped on radiators around the house.** The room cannot heat and the water lands on your windows.
- **The bedroom you sleep in.** Adds litres of water to the room you breathe in all night.
- **A cold, unused room with the door open.** Slow drying, musty smell, damp spreads.
- **Against an outside wall.** Cold wall plus wet air means condensation and mould.
