Black mould on window frames and sills is almost always caused by condensation that sits there every morning. Clean the mould once, then stop the water collecting, and it will not come back.

## Step 1: Clean it properly

Open the window, put gloves on and use a mould and mildew spray. Leave it for the time on the label, then wipe with a cloth you throw away afterwards. For rubber seals, use an old toothbrush and be gentle.

[[product id="mould-spray"]]

## Step 2: Clear condensation every morning

Water sitting on the glass runs down into the frame and seals and feeds the mould. A window vacuum clears every window in a couple of minutes.

[[product id="window-vac"]]

## Step 3: Bring humidity down

If the windows are wet every morning, the room is too humid. A hygrometer shows you where you are; aim for 40 to 60%.

[[product id="hygrometer"]]

A quiet dehumidifier in the bedroom overnight is the single biggest fix.

[[product id="dehumidifier-quiet"]]

## Step 4: Let air move

- Open trickle vents at the top of the window and leave them open.
- Open curtains fully in the morning so the frame can dry.
- Keep furniture and blinds a little away from the glass.

## Step 5: Seal what is damaged

If seals have perished or silicone around the frame has gone black inside, replace it with anti-mould sealant.

[[product id="mould-sealant"]]

## Quick checklist

- Mould spray, gloves, window open
- Window vac every morning
- Trickle vents open
- Humidity under 60%
- Curtains open in the day
- Replace black silicone
