For most homes, a quiet compressor dehumidifier of around 10 to 12 litres a day is the right buy. If the room is cold and unheated, choose a desiccant model instead. If the whole house is damp or you dry a lot of washing indoors, go up to 20 litres.

## Do you actually need one?

A dehumidifier earns its place if you see two or more of these: windows streaming every morning, a musty smell in wardrobes, washing that takes days to dry, or black spots on window frames and ceilings. A cheap hygrometer settles it. Indoor humidity that sits above 60% for long stretches is where condensation and mould take hold. The aim is 40 to 60%.

## Compressor or desiccant

**Compressor** dehumidifiers are the most efficient choice in a heated home. They work like a small fridge, cooling the air so the water drops out. Their weakness is cold rooms: below about 15°C they slow down noticeably.

**Desiccant** dehumidifiers pull water out with an absorbent wheel. They keep working in cold, unheated rooms, garages and boxrooms, are lighter and quieter, and give off a little warmth. They use more electricity, so they make most sense where a compressor model would struggle.

## What size

| Home | Look for |
|---|---|
| Bedroom or small flat | 10 to 12 litres a day |
| Average house, some laundry indoors | 12 to 20 litres a day |
| Large or very damp house, lots of laundry | 20 litres a day or more |

The litres-a-day figure is measured in warm, very humid test conditions, so real-world collection is lower. Buy for the room you have, with a little headroom.

## Getting the most from it

- Put it in the room with the problem and close the door. A dehumidifier in the hall cannot keep up with a bedroom.
- Set it to 50 to 55% and let auto mode do the rest, rather than running it flat out.
- For washing, put the airer and the dehumidifier in the same small room with the door shut. Laundry mode dries a load far faster than an open room.
- Keep it a hand's width from the wall so air can flow, and empty or plumb the tank so it does not stop overnight.
- Pair it with a timer plug if you want it running only while washing dries.
