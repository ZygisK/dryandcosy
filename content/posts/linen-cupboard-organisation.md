A good linen cupboard is quiet: everything folded the same way, stored in sets, with nothing damp or musty. It takes one afternoon to set up and five minutes a week to keep.

## 1. Edit first

Take everything out. Keep two sets of bedding per bed and two or three towels per person. Donate or recycle the rest. Less linen breathes better and stays fresher.

## 2. Fold in sets

Fold the duvet cover, fitted sheet and one pillowcase, then tuck them inside the second pillowcase. One parcel per bed, nothing to hunt for.

## 3. Store in baskets

Natural baskets keep the shelves calm and let air move around the linen, unlike plastic boxes that trap damp.

## 4. Keep the damp out

Linen cupboards are often on outside walls or near the hot water tank, and both can make linen musty. Make sure everything is bone dry before it goes in, and put a moisture trap on the bottom shelf.

[[product id="moisture-trap"]]

## 5. A little scent

A lavender sachet on each shelf, or a jar of bicarbonate of soda with a few drops of lavender oil, keeps everything smelling clean.

[[product id="lavender-sachets"]]

[[product id="bicarb"]]

## Weekly five minutes

- Refold anything that has been pulled out.
- Air the cupboard with the door open for an hour.
- Check nothing went in damp.
