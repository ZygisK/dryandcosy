## Tips for buying

- **Order early.** Heated throws and electric blankets often sell out in the first cold snap of December.
- **Check the controller.** For a couple, a dual-control electric blanket means nobody argues about the setting.
- **Pair a big gift with a small one.** A heated throw with a candle and a mug is a whole night in.
