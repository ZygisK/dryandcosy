A musty wardrobe usually means damp air is sitting in a cold corner with no circulation. It is common in winter, especially on outside walls. The fix is to let air move, take moisture out, and keep the room drier overall.

## Why it happens

- The wardrobe stands against a cold outside wall, so moisture condenses behind it.
- Clothes go in slightly damp.
- The bedroom is humid overnight and the doors stay shut.

## 1. Pull it off the wall

Move the wardrobe five to ten centimetres away from the wall so air can flow behind it. Check the wall for black spots and clean any mould with a proper spray.

[[product id="mould-spray"]]

## 2. Moisture traps inside

Moisture trap tubs pull water out of the air in enclosed spaces. Put one in the bottom of each wardrobe and replace the refill when it fills.

[[product id="moisture-trap"]]

## 3. Never put clothes away damp

Even slightly damp clothes put a lot of moisture into a closed wardrobe. Make sure everything is fully dry, especially jumpers and jeans.

## 4. Fix the room's humidity

If the bedroom sits above 60% humidity, the wardrobe will keep getting damp. A hygrometer tells you, and a quiet dehumidifier fixes it.

[[product id="hygrometer"]]

[[product id="dehumidifier-quiet"]]

## 5. Air it weekly

Open the doors for an hour once a week with the window cracked. Simple, and it works.

## Getting the smell out of clothes

Rewash in warm water with a little white vinegar in the softener drawer, dry fully, and they will be fine.
