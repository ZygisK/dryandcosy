The cosiest living rooms in winter do two things at once: they look warm and they actually stay warm. Layer the light, soften every surface, and stop the heat escaping, and you can keep the thermostat lower all evening.

## 1. Layer the light

Use three or four light sources at different heights: a floor lamp, a table lamp, candles or LED candles. Warm white bulbs everywhere.

[[product id="led-bulbs"]]

## 2. Throws and cushions

A heavy throw on the sofa and a few textured cushions change the feel of a room instantly. Make one of them a heated throw for cold nights.

[[product id="heated-throw"]]

## 3. A rug on hard floors

A big rug with underlay makes the room feel warmer underfoot and quieter. Wooden floors lose heat through the gaps too.

[[product id="floor-gap"]]

## 4. Thermal curtains, closed at dusk

Floor-length lined curtains make the room feel enclosed and hold the heat in. Tuck them behind the radiator, not over it.

[[product id="thermal-curtains"]]

## 5. Stop the door draught

A living room door with a gap underneath pulls cold air across the floor all evening.

[[product id="draught-excluder"]]

## 6. Block an unused fireplace

A fireplace you never light is a big hole in the room. A chimney balloon stops the cold pouring down it.

[[product id="chimney-balloon"]]

## Cosy living room checklist

- Three or more lamps, warm bulbs
- Heavy throw and textured cushions
- A big rug on hard floors
- Thermal curtains closed at dusk
- Draught excluder at the door
- Chimney stopper if you have an unused fireplace
