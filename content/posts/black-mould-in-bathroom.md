Bathroom mould comes back because cleaning removes the mould but not the moisture feeding it. Clean it properly once, then cut the water left behind after every shower, and most bathrooms stay clear.

## Step 1: Clean what is there

Open the window, put gloves on, and use a proper mould and mildew spray rather than plain bleach. Bleach whitens the surface but often leaves the roots in porous grout and silicone.

1. Spray the area and leave it for the time on the label.
2. Scrub grout lines with an old toothbrush.
3. Rinse and dry the surface completely.
4. Repeat on stubborn patches a day later.

## Step 2: Replace silicone that stays black

If the sealant around the bath or shower is still black after two cleans, the mould is inside it. Cut it out and reseal with anti-mould silicone. It takes an hour and lasts years.

Grout that has gone grey but is not mouldy can be refreshed with a grout pen.

## Step 3: Get the water out after every shower

This is the part that actually stops mould.

- Squeegee the tiles and screen after every shower. It takes ten seconds.
- Run the extractor fan for at least 15 minutes afterwards, door shut, window cracked.
- Hang towels to dry outside the bathroom if you can.
- Leave the shower door or curtain open so the tray dries.

[[product id="shower-squeegee"]]

## Step 4: Fix weak ventilation

A fan that barely moves a tissue is not doing its job. A fan with a humidity sensor switches itself on when the room gets steamy and keeps running until the air is dry, so nobody has to remember.

[[product id="extractor-humidistat"]]

No fan, or no window? A compact dehumidifier in the bathroom or on the landing outside it does the same job.

[[product id="bath-dehumidifier"]]

## Mould on the ceiling or walls

Clean it the same way, let it dry fully, then repaint with anti-mould bathroom paint. Normal emulsion over mould just hides it for a few weeks.

[[product id="anti-mould-paint"]]

## The five minute weekly routine

1. Quick spray of mould remover on grout and silicone corners.
2. Wipe the window frame and sill.
3. Check the fan pulls a tissue flat against the grille.
4. Wash the bath mat and shower curtain.
5. Empty the dehumidifier tank if you use one.

## Common questions

**Is black mould in the bathroom dangerous?**
Small patches are common, but mould can irritate airways, especially for people with asthma or allergies. Clean it early and fix the moisture.

**Why does mould keep coming back on silicone?**
The spores grow into the sealant itself. Once that happens, replacing the silicone is the only lasting fix.
