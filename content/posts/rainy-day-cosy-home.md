A wet, grey day is the best excuse to make home feel like a refuge. You do not need to turn the heating up. Soft light, warm textures and a dry, draught-free room do most of the work.

## 1. Light it low and warm

Turn the big light off and use lamps and warm white bulbs. A grey day feels cosier the moment the light is soft.

[[product id="led-bulbs"]]

## 2. A warm spot by the window

Pull a chair to the window, add a cushion and a heavy throw, and watch the rain. A heated throw keeps you warm without heating the room.

[[product id="heated-throw"]]

## 3. Keep the draughts out

Rain often comes with wind. A draught excluder at the door and curtains drawn early keep the room warm and quiet.

[[product id="draught-excluder"]]

[[product id="thermal-curtains"]]

## 4. Something warm to hold

A hot water bottle with a soft cover on your lap, a pot of tea, a good book. Nearly free comfort.

[[product id="hot-water-bottle"]]

## 5. Keep the house dry

Rainy days mean washing dries inside and windows steam up. A dehumidifier keeps the air fresh and the house from feeling damp and cold.

[[product id="dehumidifier-quiet"]]

## Rainy day checklist

- Lamps on, big light off
- A chair by the window with a throw
- Draught excluder at the door
- Curtains closed early
- Hot water bottle and tea
- Dehumidifier on if washing is drying
