A weekend of small jobs before the cold sets in means a warmer house, lower bills and fewer nasty surprises in January. Work down the list and tick them off.

## Heating

1. **Bleed the radiators.** Cold at the top means trapped air and wasted heat.
2. **Fit reflector panels** behind radiators on outside walls.
3. **Check the thermostat and timer.** Heat when you are home, not all day.
4. **Consider smart radiator valves** so empty rooms stay cool.

## Draughts

5. **Brush strip** on the bottom of every external door.
6. **Letterbox brush and keyhole cover.**
7. **Chimney stopper** in any fireplace you do not use.
8. **Window film** on single glazing or draughty old windows.

## Pipes and water

9. **Lag exposed pipes** in the attic, garage and under the sink. A burst pipe is the most expensive winter problem there is.
10. **Find your stopcock** and make sure it turns.

[[product id="pipe-lagging"]]

## Damp

11. **Check humidity** in bedrooms. Over 60% most mornings means condensation and mould are coming.
12. **Plan where washing dries** this winter: one room, door shut, window cracked or a dehumidifier.

## Printable version

- Bleed radiators
- Reflector panels on outside walls
- Thermostat and timer set
- Smart valves in unused rooms
- Door brush strips
- Letterbox and keyhole covers
- Chimney stopper
- Window film where needed
- Pipes lagged
- Stopcock found
- Humidity checked
- A plan for drying washing
