A few glass bottles, a big jug of white vinegar, a box of bicarbonate of soda and two essential oils cover most of the cleaning in a home. They are cheap, they smell clean rather than chemical, and they look calm on a shelf.

## What you need

- Two or three amber glass spray bottles
- White vinegar
- Bicarbonate of soda
- Tea tree oil and lavender oil
- A natural bristle brush

[[product id="glass-spray-bottle"]]

## 1. Streak-free glass and mirrors

- 1 cup water
- 1 cup white vinegar
- 1 tbsp lemon juice

Spray, then wipe with a dry microfibre or newspaper. Not for marble, granite or other natural stone, as the acid etches it.

[[product id="white-vinegar"]]

## 2. Mildew spray for windows and bathrooms

- 2 cups water
- 1 tsp tea tree oil
- Shake before every use

Spray on window seals, tile corners and silicone, leave for an hour, then wipe. Good for light mildew and keeping it away. For heavy black mould use a proper mould remover first.

[[product id="tea-tree-oil"]]

## 3. Grout and sink paste

- 3 tbsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tbsp water

Mix to a paste, work it into grout or around taps with a bristle brush, leave ten minutes and rinse.

[[product id="bicarb"]]

[[product id="scrub-brush"]]

## 4. Linen cupboard freshener

- A small jar of bicarbonate of soda
- 10 drops lavender oil
- A lid with a few small holes

It absorbs damp and musty smells and adds a soft lavender scent. Refresh every month.

[[product id="lavender-oil"]]

## Safety notes

- Never mix vinegar with bleach, or bleach with anything else.
- Label your bottles.
- Patch test on anything delicate first.
- Keep essential oils away from children and pets.
