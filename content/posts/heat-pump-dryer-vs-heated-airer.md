If you wash most days and have the space, a heat pump tumble dryer pays for itself in convenience and is far cheaper to run than an old condenser dryer. If you wash a few times a week or have no room, a covered heated airer plus a dehumidifier gets you most of the way for much less money upfront.

## Side by side

| | Heat pump dryer | Heated airer with cover | Dehumidifier on laundry mode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront cost | High | Low | Medium |
| Cost per load | Low | Low | Low to medium |
| Time per load | 2 to 3 hours | 3 to 6 hours | 6 to 10 hours |
| Space | Needs a permanent spot | Folds away | Small, moves room to room |
| Also dries the house | No | No | Yes |

## Heat pump tumble dryer

Heat pump dryers recycle warm air instead of heating it from scratch each time, so they use a fraction of the energy of a vented or condenser dryer. They are gentler on clothes too. The downsides are the upfront price and the space.

## Heated airer and cover

The budget option that works. A covered heated airer dries a full load in a few hours for pennies, and folds away when you are done.

## Dehumidifier on laundry mode

A 20 litre dehumidifier dries washing on a normal airer overnight and stops condensation and mould at the same time. If your home has damp problems anyway, this is the most useful single buy.

[[product id="dehumidifier-meaco-20l"]]

## Which should you choose?

- **Big family, washing daily, space to spare:** heat pump dryer.
- **Flat, couple, washing a few times a week:** heated airer with cover.
- **Damp house or wet windows:** dehumidifier, used with a normal or heated airer.
- **Want the fastest cheap option:** spin dryer first, then any of the above.
