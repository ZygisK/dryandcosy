Hygge (said roughly "hoo-ga") is a Danish and Norwegian word for a feeling of cosy, simple contentment: warm light, soft blankets, good company and nowhere you need to be. It is less about things and more about slowing down and feeling safe and warm.

## Where it comes from

Danish winters are long and dark, so making home feel warm and welcoming became part of the culture. Candles, lamps, shared meals and evenings in are at the heart of it.

## What hygge looks like at home

- **Soft, low light.** Lamps and candles instead of the big overhead light.
- **Natural, soft textures.** Wool, knits, sheepskin, wood.
- **Warmth you can feel.** A throw over your legs, warm socks, a hot drink.
- **A nook.** One chair or window seat that is always inviting.
- **Unhurried time.** A book, a film, a slow breakfast, phones away.

## Hygge vs coorie vs cwtch

Scotland has *coorie* (snuggling in, cosy and close to nature) and Wales has *cwtch* (a warm hug or a safe little space). All three are about warmth, comfort and slowing down.
