Most of us do laundry in a corner of the kitchen or a cupboard, not a dream utility room. A few simple swaps make that corner feel calm: natural materials, fewer bright plastic bottles, and a proper place for washing to dry.

## 1. Decant into glass

Laundry powder, pegs and dishwasher tablets look far calmer in glass jars with wooden lids than in boxes and bags.

[[product id="glass-jars"]]

## 2. Wooden pegs and hangers

Hardwood pegs in a jar and matching wooden hangers make the drying rail look intentional. Hanging shirts straight from the wash also means less ironing.

[[product id="wooden-pegs"]]

[[product id="wooden-hangers"]]

## 3. A soft laundry bag

Swap the plastic basket for a cotton canvas bag that hangs on a hook or the back of a door.

[[product id="laundry-bag"]]

## 4. A place to dry

A tall airer that folds flat keeps a full load off the floor in a small space.

[[product id="tiered-airer"]]

## 5. Keep the air dry

Drying indoors in a small space fills it with moisture. A dehumidifier with a laundry mode dries washing faster and stops condensation on windows and walls.

[[product id="dehumidifier-compressor"]]

## The calm laundry checklist

- Glass jars for powder and pegs
- Wooden pegs and hangers
- A cotton laundry bag
- One tall airer
- A dehumidifier for drying days
