In a small home the best clothes airer is the one that disappears when you are not using it. Go up, not out: a tall tiered airer, a rack on the wall, or a pulley on the ceiling all hold a full load without taking over the floor.

## Quick guide: which airer fits your space

| Your space | Best airer |
| --- | --- |
| Small flat, no spare room | Tall tiered airer |
| Narrow hallway or bathroom | Wall-mounted rack |
| Kitchen or stairwell with high ceiling | Ceiling pulley airer |
| Bedroom, need it gone in seconds | Over-door rack |
| No dryer and lots of washing | Heated airer with cover |

## 1. A tall tiered airer

Three or four levels stacked on top of each other give the hanging space of a big wing airer in the footprint of a chair. When you are done it folds flat and slides behind a door or a wardrobe.

## 2. A wall-mounted rack

Fold-down wall racks sit flat against the wall until you pull the arms out. They work well in bathrooms, over a bath, in hallways and in utility cupboards. Mount it near a window or an extractor fan so the moisture has somewhere to go.

[[product id="wall-airer"]]

## 3. A ceiling pulley airer

The old-fashioned favourite. Warm air rises, so washing hung near the ceiling dries faster, and it is completely out of the way. Ideal in kitchens and over stairwells where ceilings are high.

[[product id="pulley-airer"]]

## 4. An over-door rack

No drilling and no floor space at all. Hooks over a door and holds shirts, delicates and small items. A good extra for renters.

[[product id="overdoor-airer"]]

## 5. A heated airer for wet weeks

If you have no dryer and it rains all week, a tiered heated airer with a cover dries a full load in hours and still folds away.

[[product id="heated-airer-tiered"]]

## Where to put an airer in a small home

- In the smallest room with a window you can crack open, door shut.
- Near, but not touching, a radiator.
- In the bathroom with the extractor fan running.
- Not in the bedroom you sleep in, unless a dehumidifier is running.

## Keep the damp away

Drying indoors in a small space puts a lot of water into very little air. A compact dehumidifier in the same room stops condensation and that musty smell.
