A spa bathroom is mostly about calm: fewer bright bottles, soft natural textures, and a room that always feels fresh and dry. You do not need a renovation.

## 1. Decant into amber glass

Hand soap and shower products in matching amber glass bottles instantly calm the room down.

[[product id="soap-dispenser"]]

## 2. Rolled towels in a basket

Roll white or oatmeal towels and stack them in a cotton rope basket. It looks like a hotel and keeps towels within reach.

[[product id="rope-baskets"]]

## 3. Natural textures

A wooden bath tray, a bristle brush by the sink, a stem of eucalyptus in a small vase. Small natural touches do a lot.

[[product id="scrub-brush"]]

## 4. Keep it fresh and mould-free

A spa bathroom has to stay dry. Squeegee the glass and tiles after every shower and run the fan for 15 minutes.

[[product id="shower-squeegee"]]

If the fan is weak or there is no window, a compact dehumidifier keeps steam and mould away.

[[product id="bath-dehumidifier"]]

## The spa bathroom checklist

- Amber glass bottles
- Rolled towels in a rope basket
- One natural texture
- Squeegee after every shower
- Fan or dehumidifier on after showers
