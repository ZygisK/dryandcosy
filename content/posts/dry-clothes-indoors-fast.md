Clothes dry fast indoors when you do four things in order: get the water out, give every item space, add gentle warmth, and move or remove the damp air. Miss one and a load takes three days. Do all four and it is dry by evening.

## 1. Get the water out first

The biggest win, and it costs nothing. Use the highest spin the fabric allows, and run an extra spin on towels and jeans. A small spin dryer takes out far more water in a few minutes.

[[product id="spin-dryer"]]

## 2. Give everything space

Crowded clothes trap wet air between them. Leave a hand's width between items, hang shirts on hangers and put heavy things on the top rail where warm air collects.

[[product id="tiered-airer"]]

## 3. Add gentle warmth

A heated airer with a cover is the fastest cheap way to dry indoors. It dries a full load in a few hours for pennies.

[[product id="airer-cover-bundle"]]

## 4. Move or remove the damp air

Wet air around the clothes slows everything down. Either crack a window in a small room with the door shut, or run a dehumidifier on laundry mode, which also stops condensation on windows.

[[product id="dehumidifier-meaco-20l"]]

## The fast drying routine

1. Highest spin, or a spin dryer.
2. Hang straight away, never leave it in the drum.
3. Space it out on a tall airer.
4. Heated airer or dehumidifier, door shut.
5. Turn heavy items halfway through.

## What slows drying down

- Radiators covered in clothes: the room stays cold and the windows get wet.
- Drying in a cold, unused room with the door open.
- Big loads crammed onto one airer.
